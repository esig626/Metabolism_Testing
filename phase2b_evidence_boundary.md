# Phase 2B Evidence Boundary

Adopted: 2026-07-31 UTC  
Scope: any later Phase 2B synthesis using the Phase 2A and Phase 2A.1
evidence base

This protocol amendment defines what the bounded Phase 2 evidence can and
cannot support. It does not begin Phase 2B, make a novelty claim, or
recommend a grant direction. The decision and rationale are recorded in
`audit/decision_log.md`.

## A. Definitive evidence

Definitive evidence consists of:

- an inspected full-text primary paper;
- an exact theorem, proposition, lemma, section, equation, algorithm, figure
  or methodological passage recorded in the evidence row; and
- verified classifications of the method, uncertainty class and guarantee
  regime used in the synthesis claim.

Only this level may establish a substantive method or guarantee, support a
definitive comparison, or carry a load-bearing gap assessment. The claim must
remain within the assumptions and strength of the cited result.

## B. Qualified supporting evidence

Qualified supporting evidence includes abstract-only papers and records
deferred as relevant supporting, relevant adjacent, or outside the narrow
Phase 2A.1 repair. It may show that a literature family or terminology
exists and may identify a paper for later verification.

It may not:

- establish that a guarantee, converse, impossibility or uniform result
  holds;
- support an absence or novelty claim;
- establish that a candidate contribution is not covered by prior work; or
- be treated as a full-text exclusion from the broader Phase 2 evidence
  base.

Every statement using this evidence must be labelled qualified,
abstract-level, deferred, or otherwise provisional. A deferred record may be
revisited whenever it could materially affect a proposed contribution.

## C. Discovery-only evidence

Discovery-only evidence consists of metadata, search-result records,
bibliographic snippets and citation links without inspected primary text. It
may be used only to identify sources or literature families for subsequent
verification. It may not support a substantive scientific, statistical,
computational, guarantee, converse, absence or novelty conclusion.

## Permitted synthesis claims

Subject to traceable definitive evidence, Phase 2B may state that:

- generic fixed-sample composite-testing theory exists;
- generic converse and lower-bound techniques exist;
- verified methods weaken broad claims that general fixed-sample testing or
  converse theory is missing;
- verified EMU and 13C-MFA literature establishes mature tracer and
  flux-design methods; and
- computational integration remains a candidate opportunity, without
  treating integration alone as methodological novelty.

## Claims not permitted

The current evidence base does not permit Phase 2B to state that:

- no prior method exists;
- the literature or any incompletely searched family is saturated;
- the project is the first method of its kind;
- every adjacent-theory family has been exhaustively searched; or
- a deferred record cannot invalidate a proposed contribution.

The absence of a verified record in the bounded search is not evidence of
method absence. Any proposed residual contribution must be formulated
affirmatively against verified nearest prior work, and its evidentiary
limits must name relevant deferred and inaccessible records.
