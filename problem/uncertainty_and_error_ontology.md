# Uncertainty and Error Ontology

## Scope and evidence status

This ontology separates biological variability, experimental variation,
measurement uncertainty, forward-model discrepancy, and gross corruption. It
does not use “noise” as a catch-all category.

Frozen definition:

> A metabolic class is a family of joint probability distributions over
> observable isotope data generated by a specified biological state and
> experimental protocol, while admitting explicitly defined biological,
> batch, preparation, processing and instrumental variation.

The ontology is an analytic framework, not a claim that any listed source of
variation occurred in either case study. Protocol-specific assignments require
the primary manuscripts.

## Controlled disposition vocabulary

Each concrete source of variation must receive one primary disposition
relative to a declared scientific decision. The disposition is not an
intrinsic property of the variable: it depends on the target class, allowed
protocol range, and inferential claim.

- `BELONGS_INSIDE_CLASS`: variation is anticipated in the same biological
  state and protocol and is explicitly represented by a distribution, bounded
  set, stratification rule, or nuisance parameter. Merely ignoring variation
  does not put it inside the class.
- `DEFINES_SUBCLASS`: conditioning on the variable gives a scientifically or
  operationally meaningful refinement of a broader class, while the refined
  state remains within that broader family.
- `DEFINES_DIFFERENT_CLASS`: the variable changes the biological state or
  registered protocol that the decision is intended to distinguish.
- `CORRUPTS_OBSERVATION_OF_CLASS`: the biological state may be unchanged, but
  the resulting datum is not a valid observation from the intended pipeline,
  except under a separately declared corruption model.
- `UNRESOLVED`: evidence or specification is insufficient to select one of
  the preceding roles.

The disposition must include the range or event to which it applies. Routine
instrument drift inside a calibrated range and detector saturation are not the
same uncertainty source merely because both occur at the instrument.

In the worked-example tables, `UNRESOLVED` remains the class-disposition value.
Where a needed fact is absent from an inspected manuscript, the separate evidence
status is `UNKNOWN — NOT REPORTED`. These terms must not be collapsed into a new
disposition value.

## Two axes that must not be conflated

Every record should separately describe:

1. **Biological/class status:** did the underlying biological state or
   registered exposure change?
2. **Observation integrity:** did harvesting, preparation, acquisition, or
   processing preserve an interpretable observation of that state?

A changed MID can therefore reflect a biological class change, an admitted
within-class biological fluctuation, or corrupted observation. These
explanations cannot be merged into one residual-error term without changing
the scientific decision.

## Source-by-source ontology

| Source | Operational meaning | Where dependence is introduced | Disposition rule | Failure or reclassification trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Biological variation within a class | Variation among valid biological experimental units that share the registered class definition, such as allowed variation in growth state, pool size, or latent pathway activity | A culture-level biological state can affect every metabolite, fragment, isotopologue, and injection derived from that culture | `BELONGS_INSIDE_CLASS` only when its admissible range or distribution and sharing structure are declared; a measured stratum may `DEFINE_SUBCLASS` | A change outside the registered class range or in a class-defining variable may `DEFINE_DIFFERENT_CLASS`; without a declared range use `UNRESOLVED` |
| Biological class change | A change in the target biological state, such as a registered genotype, cell identity, culture configuration, perturbation, or environmental state | The changed state propagates through the whole network-coupled MID profile | `DEFINES_DIFFERENT_CLASS` when it is the target distinction; it may `DEFINE_SUBCLASS` under a broader parent question | If the change was unintended, undocumented, or caused by sample substitution, observation validity is also unresolved or corrupted |
| Cell-population heterogeneity | Coexisting subpopulations or cell states whose isotope observations are pooled or mixed | Cells sharing a culture are clustered; a bulk MID is an aggregate or mixture and does not reveal independent cell-level draws | `BELONGS_INSIDE_CLASS` if a mixture distribution or allowed composition range is explicit; `DEFINES_SUBCLASS` if composition is stratified; `DEFINES_DIFFERENT_CLASS` if composition is the target; otherwise `UNRESOLVED` | An unintended foreign population or cross-contamination ordinarily `CORRUPTS_OBSERVATION_OF_CLASS` |
| Batch variation | Factors shared by units processed in a common culture, media, tracer, harvesting, preparation, derivatisation, acquisition, or processing episode | All descendants of the batch share latent effects; different batch types may be crossed or nested | `BELONGS_INSIDE_CLASS` as a declared nuisance only if batches are identified and their sharing or crossing is represented; a site, lot, or run regime may `DEFINE_SUBCLASS` | Complete class–batch confounding makes biological attribution `UNRESOLVED`; a failed batch outside acceptance criteria may corrupt observations |
| Culture variation | Vessel- or independently seeded culture-level differences such as density trajectory, passage history, growth phase, microenvironment, or exposure implementation | All fractions and measurements from one culture share the culture state | `BELONGS_INSIDE_CLASS` within predeclared tolerances; a culture regime may `DEFINE_SUBCLASS`; an intended change of medium, environment, or exposure may `DEFINE_DIFFERENT_CLASS` | Infection, mislabelling, unregistered exposure failure, or gross viability failure ordinarily corrupts observation |
| Preparation variation | Harvest timing, quench delay, fractionation, recovery, extraction efficiency, evaporation, storage, or transfer effects | Shared by all fragments arising from the same prepared material; split aliquots remain linked to their parent culture | `BELONGS_INSIDE_CLASS` only through a declared preparation nuisance model; an SOP or site may `DEFINE_SUBCLASS` | Loss, swap, leakage, selective degradation, or protocol deviation beyond acceptance limits `CORRUPTS_OBSERVATION_OF_CLASS` |
| Derivatisation variation | Variation in reaction completeness, reagent lot, timing, temperature, side products, or derivative stability | A derivatisation episode can jointly shift all isotopologues or fragments derived from the aliquot | Usually `BELONGS_INSIDE_CLASS` as declared measurement-stage nuisance or `DEFINES_SUBCLASS` by protocol or lot | Failed or selectively incomplete derivatisation without an adequate model corrupts the observation |
| Instrument variation | Run-level sensitivity, mass accuracy, retention, fragmentation, detector response, carryover, source condition, tuning, or drift | Signals within an injection or run share instrument state; run order and common calibration create cross-sample dependence | `BELONGS_INSIDE_CLASS` if the instrument mode, calibration range, and run effects are represented; platform or mode may `DEFINE_SUBCLASS` | Saturation, unresolved interference, severe drift, carryover, acquisition failure, or sample swap generally corrupts observation |
| Peak-processing uncertainty | Uncertainty from peak detection, deconvolution, integration boundaries, interference handling, censoring, and manual intervention | All isotopologue areas calculated from a shared chromatogram and processing configuration are coupled | `BELONGS_INSIDE_CLASS` only if algorithm, settings, and uncertainty are part of the observation model; an algorithm or version may `DEFINE_SUBCLASS` | Incorrect assignment, unresolved coelution, inappropriate integration, or post hoc selective editing can corrupt the derived observation |
| Correction and normalisation uncertainty | Uncertainty induced by isotope-abundance correction, tracer-purity correction, response correction, blank subtraction, closure, and scaling | A correction matrix mixes peaks; MID normalisation forces components to share a denominator and creates compositional dependence | `BELONGS_INSIDE_CLASS` if correction inputs and their uncertainty are declared; a correction procedure may `DEFINE_SUBCLASS` | Wrong formula, atom count, correction matrix, tracer enrichment, blank, or normalisation denominator can corrupt the MID |
| Model discrepancy | Mismatch between the declared network, atom-routing, or observation map and the physical data-generating process, including omitted reactions, wrong atom mapping, wrong compartment assignment, or unmodelled measurement response | Discrepancy can be shared across cultures, conditions, fragments, or structural alternatives rather than being independent residual variation | `BELONGS_INSIDE_CLASS` only as an explicitly bounded nuisance or discrepancy family; an enumerated alternative may `DEFINE_SUBCLASS` or `DEFINE_DIFFERENT_CLASS`; otherwise `UNRESOLVED` | An unrestricted discrepancy class can make competing law families overlap and make uniform discrimination undefined or impossible; it must not be silently absorbed into measurement variance |
| Contamination and gross error | Foreign cells or material, sample swap, tracer or medium mispreparation, failed quench, gross carryover, saturated or corrupted acquisition, or data-file mismatch | Often affects an entire culture, preparation, plate, batch, or run and can therefore create clusters of aberrant observations | Ordinarily `CORRUPTS_OBSERVATION_OF_CLASS`; it belongs inside an analysis only through a separately declared contamination or gross-error model | If the contamination state is itself scientifically targeted it may define another class, but this must be registered rather than inferred after observing the data |

## Random variation, systematic bias, and gross failure

For every uncertainty record, distinguish:

- **Random variation:** repeated valid units vary around the class law.
- **Systematic bias:** a shared process shifts observations in one direction or
  alters the observation map; replication at a lower level does not average it
  away.
- **Gross failure:** the observation leaves the acceptance region of the
  registered pipeline or its provenance is broken.
- **Unknown mechanism:** the source or magnitude cannot be assigned from
  current evidence.

Calling a source random requires an identified ensemble or repeatable unit.
Calling a source systematic requires a reference scale. Neither label may be
assigned merely from a residual pattern.

## Required record for each uncertainty source

Each future case-specific entry should record:

- source identifier and plain-language description;
- pipeline stage and physical carrier;
- affected sampling unit;
- class disposition from the controlled vocabulary;
- permitted range, distribution, or uncertainty set;
- whether it is shared across cultures, batches, preparations, injections,
  fragments, or conditions;
- whether it is class-specific or common;
- whether it is observable, partially observable, or latent;
- QC indicator and acceptance threshold;
- expected downstream signature;
- whether it can be distinguished from a biological class change;
- source-manuscript location; and
- unresolved assumptions.

## Dependence rules

Until a case-specific design proves otherwise:

- observations descending from one culture are dependent through the shared
  biological state;
- observations prepared in one batch may share preparation bias;
- injections in one run may share instrument state;
- fragments and metabolites from one specimen are multivariate outcomes, not
  independent biological replicates;
- isotopologue peak areas used in one corrected MID are dependent through
  correction and normalisation;
- shared nuisance parameters must remain shared across conditions;
- cell, ion, scan, fragment, metabolite, peak, and MID counts must not be used
  as independent replicate counts;
- independent-product testing results apply only after the independent units
  and shared nuisance structure have been established.

These cautions are consistent with
`analyses/fixed_sample_composite_testing_foundations.md`, “Precise
EMU-induced testing problem,” which introduces independence conditionally
(“under independent sampling”) and explicitly notes that shared nuisance
prevents replacement of the joint family by an unconstrained blockwise
Cartesian product.

## Unresolved experimental questions

Source review resolved the principal biological contrasts, fixed media/tracer
settings, 48 h endpoint, major preparation and acquisition workflows, and selected
MIDs. Remaining uncertainty concerns the range and sharing of variation rather than
mere source access: replicate ancestry, stage-specific batches, variance components,
exact correction/normalisation, fragments/atom maps, missingness, QC gates, and
raw-to-MID provenance. See
[`source_information_gaps.md`](source_information_gaps.md) for the itemised
study-specific evidence locations and mathematical consequences.
