# Curated corpus

This directory is reserved for the canonical curated paper and software evidence tables defined by the review protocol.

During the structural inventory, no single current canonical corpus table was identifiable among the tracked root files. Historical `papers` CSV snapshots are preserved under `archive/migrations/data/backups/`, while Phase 2A.1 corpus-row proposals and their handoff are retained under `audit/phase2a1/` because they are proposals and workflow records rather than an unambiguous current corpus.

This is therefore a visible inventory gap rather than something filled by inference during reorganisation. The next audit should determine whether the canonical corpus is absent, contained in an archive not yet unpacked, or reconstructible from the verified screening and migration records.
