## Cross-gap interpretation

- P0047 establishes direct biological isotope Bayesian model averaging, so
  posterior structural inference cannot be used as the residual for GAP-01.
- P0048–P0051 establish adjacent biochemical stimulus, max–min,
  posterior-predictive and control-input design, so a metabolic application
  alone does not establish a theoretical gap.
- P0049 and S0017 separate an established robust biochemical design method
  and implementation from the still-unverified finite-sample EMU
  certification target.
- No full-review survival status is assigned. Phase 2A.1 weakens GAP-01 and
  GAP-03 after finding established general theory; the other registered gaps
  remain weakened.

For every row, the five contribution layers are recorded explicitly in
`synthesis/candidate_gaps.md`: established generic theory, verified
computational boundary, biological/application assumptions,
software-integration work, and unresolved/deferred evidence. In particular,
“no verified platform” in GAP-07 is bounded to the curated S0001–S0017
evidence and is not a universal software-absence claim.
