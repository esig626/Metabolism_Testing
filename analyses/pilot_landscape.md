## Corrected interpretation

Confidence/posterior uncertainty is not decision-error certification.
Sampled flux-map robustification is not uniform robustness. Verified
identifiability/rank obstructions (P0005, P0018) are not finite-sample
sample-complexity converses. P0026-P0028 provide consequential adjacent
lower-bound and asymptotic results, but their simple-hypothesis or
exponential-family assumptions do not transfer automatically to EMU-induced
composite observation families.
