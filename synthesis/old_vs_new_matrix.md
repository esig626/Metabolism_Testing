contribution” identifies the registered comparison target, not an endorsed

verified”—is bounded to the cited inspected sources or the current curated
corpus. It means only that those sources do not establish the capability or
result; it is not a universal absence claim. Administrative title/abstract
dispositions and search stopping are not used to infer that a method family
is exhausted.

## Interpretation boundary

The matrix distinguishes three claims that must not be collapsed:

1. a design criterion can be implemented;
2. a test controls a stated error under stated assumptions; and
3. an experiment is certified sufficient, or proved insufficient, for a
   declared mechanism decision.

Verified prior work is extensive at the first level and supplies important
special cases at the second and third. The remaining rows concern narrower
conjunctions of EMU structure, nuisance, block design and guarantees; none is
labelled novel.

## Phase 3B direct-MID addendum

This addendum is an evidence-boundary update, not a grant recommendation and
not a novelty claim. It adds direct-MID prior art that the generic Phase 2A.1
matrix did not resolve. Where an earlier row describes the residual as
“computational adaptation,” the direct-MID reading is now conditional: first
construct the actual culture-induced law family and prove that the cited
generic theorem applies. Without that proof, the residual may include a
representation/applicability result and must not be called purely
computational.

### Phase 3B interpretation boundary

The current evidence supports an actual theoretical programme—family
construction, separation/overlap analysis, theorem applicability and
hierarchy-aware finite-sample design—but does not establish that programme as
novel. It may contract after stronger prior art or an applicability proof is
found. Conversely, a generic theorem does not close it until its assumptions
are verified for the experiment-induced family.
